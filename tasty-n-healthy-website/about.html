How We Started

From one small kitchen to your neighborhood favorite

Tasty n Healthy opened its doors as a small counter-service kitchen with a simple frustration: "healthy" food nearby usually meant sad, wilted, or bland. We set out to prove that fresh, whole-food meals could be exciting, craveable, and ready in minutes.

Every recipe on our menu is developed and tested in-house. We work directly with local farms for produce, hand-make our dressings and sauces daily, and never take shortcuts with fryers, syrups, or preservatives.
